Inbox Automation

A small personal tool that helps its developer manage their own Gmail inbox with the help of an AI assistant.

What it does

Inbox Automation connects to one Gmail account, the developer's own, through the Gmail API. With the account owner's permission it can:

  • read and search the owner's emails and threads,
  • organize them, for example by applying labels, archiving, or marking messages as read, and
  • prepare draft replies for the owner to review.

An AI assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic) is used to summarize messages and write those drafts. The owner reviews every draft before anything is sent.

Who can use it

Inbox Automation is not a public service. There is no sign-up and it is not offered to other people. Only the developer authorizes it, for the developer's own Google account.

Google user data

The tool requests three Gmail permissions: read-only access to messages (gmail.readonly), the ability to create drafts (gmail.compose), and the ability to organize messages, such as applying labels or archiving them (gmail.modify). How that data is handled is described in the Privacy Policy.
